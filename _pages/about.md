*Insights and intelligence from [`newtroubadours.org`](https://newtroubadours.org).*

To a large extent, the UK traditional storytelling and touring grass roots music scenes can be representend as small world networks, with a high degree of interconnection between geographically disparate stroytelling clubs and grass roots music venues and promoters.

The New Troubadours *Intelligence* blog seeks to capture the pulse of UK storytelling and grass roots music scenes, providing insights and intelligence to performers, promoters, clubs and venues.
